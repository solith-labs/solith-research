# Solith Research

Specialized knowledge base and research repository for autonomous crypto intelligence systems.

## Objective

Build a structured, technical foundation of research and experimentation to support the development of Solith — an autonomous platform that detects early crypto narratives, memes, and on-chain signals using AI agents.

## Research Domains

- Narrative and meme detection using social and on-chain signals
- Multi-modal data fusion between off-chain text and on-chain events
- Lightweight, tool-using AI agent architectures for real-time intelligence
- Signal scoring, ranking, and evaluation frameworks
- Latency, noise filtering, and false-positive mitigation in early detection systems
- Integration of conviction market primitives with agentic verification

## Repository Structure

- `/narrative-detection` — Research and experiments on early narrative and meme identification
- `/data-fusion` — Studies and pipelines combining social and on-chain data
- `/agent-architectures` — Design notes and prototypes for autonomous intelligence agents
- `/evaluation` — Metrics, backtesting approaches, and signal quality frameworks
- `/notes` — General research notes and literature summaries

## Status

Active research phase. Content is added incrementally as ideas and experiments develop.
